Vigta Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · effective 7 October 2026

Vigta is a to-do app that sorts your tasks by value and effort. This policy explains what information Vigta processes, why, for how long and who helps us. It applies to Vigta on iPhone, Android and the web (vigta.app).

In short:

  • You can use Vigta without an account. Your tasks then stay on your phone.
  • You only need an account to sync between devices or share lists. Your data is then stored in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • We never sell your information, show no ads and use no advertising ID.
  • Speech in Voice Capture (Indtal) is turned into text on the phone itself. Vigta does not record or store audio.
  • Statistics and research use numbers only, never the text of your tasks.
  • You can delete your account and all your data in the app or at vigta.app.

2. Without an account: your data stays on your phone

If you use Vigta without signing in, your areas, lists, tasks, notes, assessments and photos are stored only on your device. We have no account for you and cannot see your data.

The only thing that can leave the phone is crash reports without content, and only if you said yes at first launch (section 5.1). You can change this under Settings › Privacy.

Some features use information that stays on the phone, and we never receive it:

  • Calendar: if you allow access, Vigta reads your calendar on the phone to show events next to your tasks. Calendar data is not sent to us.
  • Apple Reminders (iPhone only): if you import from Reminders, they are read on the phone and become tasks in Vigta.
  • Reminders and the daily overview: are local notifications shown by the phone itself.
  • The log of completed tasks: titles, notes and list names in the log always stay on the phone.

3. Voice Capture: speaking your tasks

Voice Capture lets you speak your tasks. You can always type instead, so no feature in Vigta requires you to speak.

  • Speech is recognised by the phone's own speech recognition, on the phone. On iPhone, Vigta uses Apple's recognition and requires it to run on the device. On Android, Vigta only uses recognition that runs on the device. If your phone does not have it, Voice Capture is not available and you can type instead.
  • Vigta does not send audio to us or anyone else, and Vigta does not store recordings.
  • What is stored is the text that comes out of the recognition: the task's title, note, date and list, exactly as if you had typed it. The text follows the same rules as your other tasks.
  • Read-back in Voice Capture uses the phone's own voices, on the phone.
  • Vigta asks for access to the microphone and speech recognition the first time you use Voice Capture. You can remove access in the phone's settings.

4. With an account: sync and sharing

4.1 Account and sign-in

You create an account when you turn on sync, share a list or accept an invitation. You sign in with Apple, Google or a one-time code sent to your email. Vigta has no passwords.

We process:

  • Email and user ID, for account, sign-in and sharing. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only receive Apple's relay address.
  • Name, which you give when you create the account. It is pre-filled from Apple or Google, and you can change it. Your name is shown to people you share lists with and to your contacts in Vigta. Your email is shown only to the owner of a list you are a member of.
  • Sign-in emails (one-time codes) are sent from noreply@mail.vigta.app through our email provider Brevo.

4.2 Your content

When you are signed in, your areas, lists, tasks, notes, subtasks, assessments, comments and photos are stored and synced so they are the same on all your devices and on the web.

  • Photos and attachments are stored in our storage with a limit per account: 100 MB on the free plan and 2 GB with Plus.
  • The log of completed tasks is synced without text: only ID, outcome, times, stars, category and whether the deadline was met. Titles and notes in the log stay on the phone.
  • We use the content only to provide the feature to you and the people you share with. We do not read it unless you ask us for help with something specific, or the law requires it.

4.3 Sharing and invitations

  • If you share a list, we store the email and name of the people you share with (your contacts in Vigta) so they can get access.
  • If you invite by link, only an encrypted version (hash) of the link code is stored. The link can be used once and expires after 14 days, or when you withdraw it.
  • The recipient must accept an invitation before becoming a member.
  • All members of a shared list can see its tasks according to their role: owner, editor or viewer.

You are responsible for what you write about other people in tasks, notes, comments and photos. Do not put sensitive information about others (for example health) in a shared list.

4.4 Cross-functional prioritisation and Shared prioritisation

On shared lists, members can assess tasks individually and together.

  • Shared prioritisation: the owner and editors each give their assessment (stars and a short comment of up to 280 characters). Viewers see the result.
  • Cross-functional prioritisation: the owner chooses who assesses what, sets a deadline and can approve the final assessment. Assessments, explanations and comments are visible to the members the list's rules allow. By default you do not see others' answers until you have answered yourself or the deadline has passed. The rules are enforced on our server, not only in the app.
  • If you leave a list or are removed, your group assessments on that list are deleted.
  • If you delete your account, your explanations and comments are deleted. In Cross-functional prioritisation your stars remain on the list but are shown as "Former member" without your name, so the team's joint assessment does not fall apart.
  • The combined category is calculated in the app and not stored. Vigta makes no decisions about you. The sorting is a suggestion you control.

4.5 Push notifications

For Cross-functional prioritisation and invitations, Vigta can send push notifications: an invitation, a deadline reminder and a notice that an assessment is ready. For this we store your device's push token, which is deleted when you sign out. Notifications are sent through Apple Push Notification service (iPhone) and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android). On the lock screen only the list name and number of tasks are shown, never assessments or comments. You can turn push off in the phone's settings.

Other events in a shared list, such as new comments, are only shown as a counter in the app. They are kept for 12 months.

4.6 Profile information (optional)

After your first sign-in you can choose to give company size, industry, field and your role from fixed lists. It is optional, you can skip it and change or delete it under Profile. We never ask for the name of your company. The information is used only in aggregate figures, and only when at least 50 people are behind each figure.

5. Crash reports, statistics and research

5.1 Crash reports (only if you say yes)

If you say yes at first launch, Vigta sends crash and performance reports to Sentry, which stores them in the EU (Germany). Reports contain phone model, operating system, app version and language, but no task text, no user ID and no IP address. They are deleted after 30 days.

5.2 Usage statistics (only if you say yes)

If you tick "Share anonymous usage statistics" at sign-in, Vigta sends counts of which features are used and how often. These are counts, never text. The box is not ticked in advance.

On the server we also derive whether people return after 1, 7 and 30 days, and how far they got in onboarding (first task, first assessment, sign-in). We do this from sync timestamps we already have, and we collect nothing extra from the phone.

Product analytics in the app and on the web happens only with your consent, through PostHog, which stores data in the EU (Frankfurt). No advertising ID and no third-party cookies are used.

Raw statistics are kept for 13 months. After that only aggregate figures exist, which cannot be traced back to you.

5.3 Research on prioritisation

Exekio does research on how people estimate and prioritise better. If you have an account, we use a pseudonymised copy of the numbers about your completed tasks (outcome, times, stars, category, whether the deadline was met) and which assessment template you used. The copy never contains titles, notes or other text. Your user ID is replaced by a code, and the key to the code is kept separately.

This is based on our legitimate interest in research. You are told at sign-in, and you can opt out at any time under Settings › Privacy › Contribute to research. If you opt out, your raw research data is deleted. Raw research data is kept for 13 months, then only aggregate figures.

6. Importing from other apps

You can bring your tasks over from Google Tasks, Microsoft To Do, Todoist, TickTick, Any.do and Apple Reminders. Import only happens when you start it, and Vigta only asks for read access where the other app requires access. Vigta never changes or deletes anything in the other app.

  • Google Tasks: you grant access through Google (read access to Tasks plus your name and email to show which account you are importing from).
  • Microsoft To Do: you grant access through Microsoft (read access to To Do and your basic profile).
  • Todoist: you grant access through Todoist, or you choose a backup file you downloaded from Todoist yourself. The file is read on the phone.
  • TickTick and Any.do: you choose a file or paste text that you exported from the app yourself. It is read on the phone, and Vigta gets no access to your account in the other app.
  • Apple Reminders: read on iPhone (section 2).

What we fetch is lists, tasks, dates, subtasks, notes and (where the source has them) recurrence and priority. It becomes ordinary tasks in Vigta and then follows the same rules as your other tasks: on the phone, and in our EU storage if you sync.

Access is used only during the import. Vigta does not store the access on our servers and does not fetch anything again unless you start a new import. You can revoke access:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Microsoft: account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or myapps.microsoft.com (work and school accounts)
  • Todoist: under Settings › Integrations in Todoist

6.1 Data from Google

Vigta's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice:

  • Data from Google is used only to import your tasks into Vigta and show them to you and the people you choose to share a list with.
  • It is transferred to others only when needed to provide that feature (our service providers in section 8, under a data processing agreement), for security, or when the law requires it.
  • It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never transferred to advertising platforms or data brokers.
  • It is never used for statistics, research or marketing.
  • Data from Google Workspace APIs, including Google Tasks, is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine learning models.
  • No person at Vigta reads it unless you explicitly ask us to, it is necessary for security, or the law requires it.

The same applies, without exception, to data from Microsoft, Todoist, TickTick and Any.do.

7. Buying Plus

Plus is bought and cancelled in the App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google process the payment and your payment details as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card details.

To give you Plus on all your devices we use RevenueCat, which receives the receipt from the store and a pseudonymous user ID (not your email). We store whether you have Plus and when it expires. The accounting records of the purchase are kept by Apple and Google.

Consent to statistics, research or profile information is never a condition for buying Plus.

10. Delete your account and data

You can delete your account in the app under Settings › Account › Delete account, or at vigta.app/delete-account without the app.

If you no longer have the app, you can also write to support@vigta.app from the email address the account was created with. We then delete the account within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

When you delete your account, we delete your sign-in user, your areas, lists and tasks with photos, your contacts, invitations, events, profile information, push tokens, purchase status, the pseudonym key and your raw research and statistics data. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke access at Apple.

  • Lists you own and have shared are deleted for all members. Before you delete, the app shows who will lose the list, and in Cross-functional prioritisation you can first choose a new owner.
  • Lists owned by others keep their content, but you are no longer a member (see section 4.4 on assessments).
  • What remains: aggregate figures that cannot be traced back to you, and proof that you withdrew a consent.

Deleting the account does not stop a Plus subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

11. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the information we hold about you
  • have incorrect information corrected
  • have your information deleted
  • have processing restricted
  • receive your data in a common format (data portability). Use Settings › Data › Export all data, which gives a JSON file with your content, consents, profile and log. You can get pseudonymised research data by writing to us.
  • object, in particular to research based on legitimate interest
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what happened before

Write to privacy@vigta.app. We reply within one month.

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark, www.datatilsynet.dk, or to the data protection authority where you live.

13. Security

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) and stored encrypted at Supabase. Access to data is controlled on the server, so people can only see what they are entitled to, including in shared lists. Access keys to other services are stored encrypted. If we discover a security breach that affects you, we notify you and the Danish Data Protection Agency as required.

14. Web and cookies

Vigta on the web uses no third-party cookies and no advertising. Browser storage is used only for what is necessary: keeping you signed in and remembering your settings. Product analytics on the web only happens if you said yes.

15. Children

Vigta is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information about children under 13. If we become aware of it, we delete the account.

16. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The current version and date are shown at the top and at vigta.app/privacy.
