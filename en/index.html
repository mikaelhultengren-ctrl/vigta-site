Do what is worth doing first.

Vigta is one app for your tasks and to-dos - at the office, in the workshop, on the shop floor and at home. You rate each task for value and effort, and Vigta shows you what to start on.

How Vigta works Vigta on the web
Coming to iPhone and Android. The web app opens at the same time.

Four categories. No guesswork.

Priorities are built on value and effort - not on whatever shouts loudest. Every task lands in one of four categories, and the rule behind it can be explained line by line.

Critical

High value, and it needs doing now.

Quick win

High value for little effort. Do these first.

Plan

High value, but it takes time. Set a day aside.

Park

Low value right now. It is there if that changes.

On your own, together or across teams

Three ways to prioritise, in the same app.

Individual prioritisation

Your own tasks, rated with stars for value and effort. Vigta sorts them so the most important one is on top.

Shared prioritisation

Share a list with your family, crew or colleagues. Everyone sees the same tasks in the same order.

Cross-functional prioritisation

Several people rate the same task on their own. Then you see the combined rating and can agree.

Your data

Vigta works without an account. If you create one to sync or share, your data is stored in the EU. We do not sell data and we do not show ads. Read the privacy policy, or delete your account.
