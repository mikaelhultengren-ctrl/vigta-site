Help

How Vigta works, and how to reach us.

Write to us

Email support@vigta.app. We reply within two working days. It helps to tell us which phone you use and what you were doing.

Getting started

  1. Tap the plus and type the task. You can save it without rating it. It then sits under Not rated.
  2. Rate it with three sliders: Importance, Effort and Consequence (the cost of waiting).
  3. Start at the top of the list. It is sorted by what is worth doing first.

Rating and categories

From your three answers, Vigta puts the task in one of four categories: Critical, Quick win, Plan or Later. In private lists they are called Urgent, Quick, Important and Later.

Tasks

  • Tap a task to open it. You can set a date and time, a reminder, repeat and a deadline, add subtasks and attach photos and files.
  • Hold a task for its menu or to move it. Hold longer to select several.
  • Swipe left on a task to delete it. A short swipe shows the button.
  • Swipe down on the home screen to search.
  • A list can be shown as a list, Matrix or Weekly plan.

Weekly plan

Weekly plan shows the dated tasks of the list on the days of the week, Monday to Sunday. Change week with the arrows at the top. The plus next to a day creates a task on that day, and you can also place tasks without a date on the day.

Calendar

Calendar shows your tasks and events by day, 3 days, week, month or as a list. The calendars already on your phone can be shown. In Settings you can connect Google Calendar or Outlook, and you can turn events off to see tasks only.

Notes

In Notes you can write, add photos and draw. You can pin a note to the top, search your notes and convert a note to a task.

Dictation

Hold the plus and say your tasks. Vigta writes them down, and you can correct them before saving. They stay under Not rated until you rate them. Dictation of tasks is available in Danish and English.

Overview

Overview shows what you have completed. You choose which tiles to show, and you can export completed tasks.

Sharing

How do I share a list?

Hold the tab of the list, choose Share and send the invitation. The person you invite gets a link, or can enter the 6-character invitation code.

Can we rate together?

Yes. In a shared list, several people can rate the same task on their own. Vigta shows the combined rating and where someone differs.

What is Cross-team?

Cross-team is for lists where ratings and deadlines are assigned to specific people. It is part of Plus.

Account, Plus and data

Do I need an account?

No. Vigta works on your phone without an account. You only need one to sync between devices, share lists or use Vigta on the web.

What do I get with Plus?

Without Plus you have lists, rating, reminders, calendar, sync and sharing with up to two people. Plus adds Cross-team, sharing with more than two people and 2 GB for photos and files. Plus is bought in the app.

Can I bring my tasks from another app?

Yes. In Settings you can import from Reminders, Todoist, Microsoft To Do, Google Tasks and others. You can also export all your data.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete account. You can do it in the app or by writing to us. Apple accounts are deleted from the app on iPhone. You cancel a Plus subscription yourself in the store.

Where is my data stored?

Without an account, only on your phone. With an account, also on our server in the EU. The details are in the privacy policy.
