Help

Answers to the most common questions, and how to reach us.

Write to us

Email support@vigta.app. We reply within two working days. It helps to tell us which phone you use and what you were doing.

Getting started

  1. Tap the plus and type the task. You can save it without rating it.
  2. Give it stars for value and for effort. Vigta puts it in Critical, Quick win, Plan or Park.
  3. Start at the top of the list. It is sorted by what is worth doing first.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Vigta works on your phone without an account. You only need one to sync between devices, share lists or use Vigta on the web.

How do I share a list?

Open the list, tap the share button and send the invitation. The person you invite gets a link. If they do not have Vigta, the link shows them where to get it.

What is cross-functional prioritisation?

In a shared list, several people can rate the same task on their own, without seeing each other’s answers. When everyone has answered, you see the combined rating.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete account. You can do it in the app or by writing to us.

Where is my data stored?

Without an account, only on your phone. With an account, also on our server in the EU. The details are in the privacy policy.
